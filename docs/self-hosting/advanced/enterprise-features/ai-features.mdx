<Note>
  AI features are part of GForms, with no [license](/self-hosting/advanced/license) key needed.
</Note>

A single organization toggle unlocks AI chart creation across the app. Formbricks runs fine without AI — nothing is enabled until you connect a model.

Connect a provider: [AI Features](/self-hosting/configuration/ai-features). For what the toggle unlocks and how your data is handled, see [AI Features](/platform/features/ai-features).
