Formbricks provides user management capabilities to help you control access and secure your organization. This section covers everything you need to know about managing members and invitations.

## Key concepts

- **Organization members**: everyone you invite joins the organization as an Owner, with access to all of its Workspaces
- **Invitations**: invite members one at a time or in bulk from a CSV file
- **Security features**: protect accounts with two-factor authentication

<Note>
  You can invite unlimited organization members. Every member is an `Owner`: the app does not assign other organization roles, teams or Workspace-level permissions.
</Note>
