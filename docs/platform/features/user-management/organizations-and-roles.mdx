An organization holds your Workspaces, their surveys and responses, and the people who work on them.

<Note>
  Every member joins the organization as an `Owner`. Invitations, individual or in bulk, always add members with
  the Owner role, and a member's role cannot be changed in the app.
</Note>

## Members

All members of the organization and their pending invitations are listed under **Settings → Organization → Teams**, each with their status. From there, owners [invite new members](/platform/features/user-management/invite-members) and remove members who no longer need access.

## Owner

- Have full access to the organization, its data, and settings
- Can manage all aspects of the organization including billing, integrations, and member management
- Can create, update and delete Workspaces and everything in them: surveys, responses, actions, tags and integrations

## Best practices

- **Invite only who needs access**: every member is an Owner, with full control over the organization and its data
- **Regular audits**: Periodically review the organization's members and remove those who no longer need access
